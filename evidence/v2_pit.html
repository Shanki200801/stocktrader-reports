Survivorship-free (v2_pit): the universe on each date is the top 500 NSE stocks by 126-day median traded value as of that date, built from official NSE bhavcopies (2010–2026), including 1,752 stocks that were ever in the top 500, many since delisted. Delisted stocks exit at their last traded price. This is the honest test. Prices exclude dividends (NSE prev-close adjusts splits/bonus/rights only), which is slightly conservative.
Headline: the equal-weight universe now earns ~12%/yr, close to the Nifty 500 index (13.5%), so the survivorship bias is gone. Plain momentum still beats both; the pre-registered trend-filtered variant does not.
