Formulas

Show all formulas
  • sigma: σ = stdev(daily r, last 252d), r_t = P_t/P_(t-1) - 1; shown annualised ×√252 (annualisation cancels in Z)
  • MR6: MR6 = (P_t / P_(t-126) - 1) / σ
  • MR12: MR12 = (P_t / P_(t-252) - 1) / σ
  • Z: Z6, Z12 = cross-sectional z-score of MR6, MR12 over the eligible universe on date t
  • WAZ: WAZ = 0.5·Z6 + 0.5·Z12
  • NMS: NMS = 1 + WAZ if WAZ ≥ 0, else 1 / (1 - WAZ)
  • PTH: PTH = P_t / max(P over last 252d) (52-week-high proximity, George & Hwang 2004)
  • Trend: trend_ok = P_t > SMA200 and SMA50 > SMA200 (Faber 2007); '+T' variants drop names failing it
  • Regime: Regime ON if Nifty 500 close > SMA200; '+T' variants hold cash when OFF
  • β: β = cov(r_i, r_m) / var(r_m), 252d window vs Nifty 500

Assumptions: A1 daily simple returns; universe is today's Nifty 500 (S1 survivorship bias). News tags are regex rules (HIGH/MEDIUM, last 7 days), not verified facts. Candidate status is from ledger/backtest_expectations.csv and may be revised.
